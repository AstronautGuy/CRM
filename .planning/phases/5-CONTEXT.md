# Phase 5 Context - Marketing & Automation Reminders

## Key Decisions

### 1. Ad Campaign Tracking & ROI Analytics
- **Campaign Data Model**: Ad network platform (`GOOGLE_ADS`, `FACEBOOK_ADS`, `LINKEDIN_ADS`, `TWITTER_ADS`), campaign name, budget, spend, clicks, impressions, and conversions.
- **ROI Analytics**: Calculate Cost Per Lead (CPL) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) integrated with deal/contact conversion counts.

### 2. Automated Task Reminders & Notifications
- **Task & Follow-up Scheduler**: Tasks linked to contacts, companies, or deals with due dates, priority (`HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW`), and assigned team member.
- **In-App & Email Notification Triggers**: Automated reminder alerts triggered when follow-ups are due or when leads remain untouched for extended periods.

### 3. Calendar & Activity Scheduler UI
- **Calendar & Timeline View**: Interactive calendar/timeline displaying scheduled follow-ups, calls, deadlines, and reminders across the team.
