# Milestone 8: Marketing, Automations & Analytics - Requirements (Archived)

## 1. Goal
Provide a comprehensive suite for Tenant Admins to automate client follow-ups, launch and track public catalogues/ads, and deeply analyze their business performance via an advanced dashboard.

## 2. Requirements

### Phase 23: Reminders & Automations
- [x] **Automated Invoice Reminders**: System to track and send emails for "Due Soon" and "Overdue" invoices.
- [x] **Abandoned Quote Follow-ups**: Automated nudges to clients who haven't accepted their public quote link.
- [x] **Custom Drip Campaigns**: Ability to schedule delayed marketing emails or sequences for specific client segments.
- [x] **Internal Staff Alerts**: Webhooks or in-app notifications prompting staff (e.g., "Action Required: Client viewed Quote 3 times").

### Phase 24: Catalogues & Ads
- [x] **Public Catalogues**: A secure, public-facing link or PDF dynamically generated from the Tenant's Products/Services database.
- [x] **Storefront Checkout**: Basic public UI allowing a client to "buy" or request a quote directly from the catalogue link.
- [x] **Ad Platform Integration**: Webhook/API connections to ingest leads directly from Meta/Google Lead Ads into the CRM.
- [x] **Ad Analytics**: CRM internal tracking of Ad campaigns, mapping leads back to their source for ROAS computation.

### Phase 25: Advanced Dashboard & Reporting
- [x] **Financial Metrics**: Widgets displaying MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue), Total Revenue, and Outstanding Balances across the organization.
- [x] **Sales Metrics**: Charts tracking Win Rates, Total Quotes Sent, and Conversion funnels.
- [x] **Marketing Metrics**: Graphs showing Leads Generated over time and Ad Spend effectiveness.
- [x] **Team Performance**: Leaderboards or tables showing internal tasks completed and revenue generated per staff member.
